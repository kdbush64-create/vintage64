Privacy Policy

Revised: February 19, 2026  ·  Fort Worth, TX

At Vintage64TX, your privacy is a core component of our security-first approach. This policy outlines how we handle data for individuals and small businesses seeking Linux migration and website services in Fort Worth, TX and the broader DFW Metroplex.

1. Data We Collect

We only collect data that is strictly necessary for providing services. This includes:

  • Identity data: Name and contact details you provide when reaching out.
  • Contact data: Email address submitted via our contact form.
  • Technical data: IP addresses and browser types collected by Cloudflare for security and performance.
  • Service data: Hardware specs or diagnostic information shared during a Phoenix Conversion or website project.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not host your sensitive payment data on our own servers. We use industry-standard partners:

  • Stripe: Processes all payments. We never see or store your credit card numbers.
  • Cloudflare: Provides hosting, CDN, and security for this website. Subject to Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

3. Texas Data Privacy (TDPSA)

In accordance with the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA):

  • No sale of data: Vintage64TX does not sell your personal or sensitive data to third parties — ever.
  • Right to access or delete: You may request a copy of your data or its deletion at any time by contacting us directly.
  • No targeted advertising: We do not use your data for third-party advertising or automated profiling.

4. Data Security

As a CompTIA Security+ certified IT provider, we implement encrypted data transfers (HTTPS), multi-factor authentication on all business tools, and strict data minimization — if we don't need it to do the job, we don't ask for it.
