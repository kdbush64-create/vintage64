THE REAL PROBLEM

Why your computer feels slow

In most cases, the hardware is fine. The software got heavy.

WHAT PILES UP OVER TIME

The clutter compounds.

Years of updates, programs that launch themselves at startup, and background services you never asked for. A computer that started in 45 seconds when it was new can take several minutes a few years later. Microsoft's answer is to sell you a new one.
