WHO AM I

Hi, I'm DeWayne.
I bring back the computer you were told to replace.

I'm based in Fort Worth, and Vintage64TX is my one-person shop. Before this, I spent more than 15 years running technology programs for large companies. Now I put that experience to more direct use: helping my neighbors and small businesses get what they actually need from technology, in plain English, without the runaround.

The computer side started because I kept seeing perfectly good machines get thrown away, not because they were broken, but because Microsoft drew a line in the sand with Windows 11. A machine that booted fine on Monday shouldn't end up in a landfill on Friday because a TPM chip is the wrong version. I started giving that hardware a second life, and the results were consistent enough that it became a real service.

The website side came from small business owners who were paying monthly platform fees for sites they didn't fully understand and couldn't fully control. A plain, hand-built website isn't a new idea. It's the original idea, and for most small businesses it's still the right one: fast, secure, no monthly software bill, and you own the files outright. You can see my work at comfortrefined.com, a boutique aesthetics studio in New Braunfels, and v64otd.com, where I write about Texas and national issues.

I take Phoenix Conversion drop-offs throughout North Fort Worth, Keller, Southlake, Roanoke, Haslet, Watauga, Grapevine and North Richland Hills. I keep only a few jobs going at a time so the quality doesn't slip. And if I'm not the right fit for what you need, I'll tell you that too.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you service computers outside of DFW?

Linux conversions are available for drop-off throughout North Fort Worth, Keller, Southlake, Roanoke, Haslet, Watauga, Grapevine, and North Richland Hills — hardware work has to be done hands-on, so it's limited to that area. Website projects are fully remote and available anywhere in the country.

What does a flat-fee quote actually mean?

It means the price is set before any work starts, and it doesn't change. No hourly clock running in the background, no extra charges at pickup. If scope changes after we've agreed on a price, we talk about it first — nothing gets added silently.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

No. I translate the technical side into plain language, explain the tradeoffs, and make sure you know what you're getting before anything is agreed. If I use a term that doesn't make sense, ask — that's what I'm here for.
